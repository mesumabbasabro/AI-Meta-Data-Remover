Frequently asked questions

Does this remove AI detection or invisible watermarks?

No. Removing file metadata does not guarantee an image cannot be identified as AI-generated. Pixel-level watermarks, visual patterns, and copies of provenance elsewhere are outside this tool's scope.

What information does this tool remove?

It strips supported metadata containers such as EXIF, XMP, comments, and common embedded AI generation parameters. It preserves image and color-profile data needed for faithful display. For JPEG, ICC profiles and Adobe color information are retained.

Are my pictures uploaded to a server?

No. The code inspects and transforms the image locally in your browser. Your images are processed locally and are not sent to our servers.

Does it work on every image?

The tool supports standard JPEG, PNG and WebP files. It preserves essential image information and rejects malformed or unsupported formats. Specialized metadata, custom formats or invisible watermarks may remain. Check the verification report after cleaning.
